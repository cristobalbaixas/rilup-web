Manifesto

We are not against artificial intelligence. We think it is a fantastic tool, driving an enormous leap in technology. We use it, we admire it and we want it to keep growing.

But in the middle of so much digital content, something is getting lost: how incredible the real world is, and what real people are actually capable of. When everything can be generated, the first thought in front of an extraordinary real achievement is “must be AI”. And right there, human merit dissolves into doubt.

Rilup is a place to celebrate what is real: human talent, effort, or simply the moment that actually happened.

A place where the incredible becomes credible again, because here it can be proven.

The rules of the game

Everything that follows exists for one reason: that what you see here is real, and that you do not have to take our word for it.

1. Everything is captured here, live.

Nothing can be imported: not from the camera roll, not from a file, not from another app. Rilup's camera, with its built-in editor, is the only way in.

2. Every capture is verified on the spot.

At capture, the file's cryptographic fingerprint is registered and Google or Apple vouch that the app and the device are genuine. If anything does not match, it is not published. That is the seal you see on every post.

3. The ORIGINAL button: nothing is hidden.

You can add ON TOP (text, cuts, adjustments), but never change what was captured. Everything is declared and can be removed: anyone can see your capture exactly as it left the sensor by pressing and holding “View original”. No makeup.

4. Reality Level: trust is earned with a track record.

Your level rises by publishing verified captures over time, never with likes or followers. Popularity does not buy trust. And if someone doubts your content and reports it, review can confirm it: your post then carries the “Real Confirmed” seal. Being accused and coming out verified is not a punishment: it is your medal, and the recognition that you did something incredible.

5. Projects: merit is also the path.

Not every achievement fits in one capture. In a project you can show the whole evolution, every step with its real date and its verification.

6. Your feed is yours.

Chronological, only from the people you follow. No algorithm decides what you deserve to see. If you want to discover new things you go to Explore, and there the interests YOU declare are in charge.

We do not promise that fooling a system is impossible; nobody honest can promise that. We promise that cheating here is expensive, gets detected and gets punished. And that when something amazes you, you can trust that someone, for real, did it.

Let AI keep advancing. Here, we celebrate the human.
