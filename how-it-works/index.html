How it works

What is behind the seal, with no smoke.

How it works

We do not detect AI. We verify origin.

Guessing which image was generated is a losing race: it gets harder every month. So Rilup does not play that game. Instead of looking at content to suspect it, we verify the provenance of every capture, at the instant it happens.

  1. You capture inside Rilup. The in-app camera is the only way in. There is no gallery, no “upload file”, no way to bring something from another app.
  2. The capture is sealed at that same instant. The file's cryptographic fingerprint is computed, a unique number that changes completely if a single pixel changes, and the device's hardware key signs it. Google (Play Integrity) or Apple (App Attest) vouch that the app is the official one, unmodified, running on a genuine device.
  3. The server checks, it does not trust. When you publish, our server recomputes the fingerprint of the file that actually arrived and compares it with the one registered at capture. If they do not match byte for byte, the file is deleted and nothing is published. None of this is validated on your phone: the server is the one checking.
  4. The time comes from clocks nobody controls. Between capture and publish there is a short window, measured against our server's clock and Google's or Apple's, not your phone's.

What this proves

  • That it came out of a real camera, on a real device.
  • That the official, unmodified app captured it.
  • That the published bytes are exactly the ones that left the sensor.
  • That the capture happened when it says it happened.

What it does not prove

  • What was in front of the lens. That is the hard limit and we say it up front: a camera verifies its own origin, not the honesty of the scene.
  • We do not promise that fooling a system is impossible; nobody honest can promise that. We promise that cheating here is expensive, gets detected and gets punished. All the limits, in plain words.

The seal

The mark, never a checkmark.

On other networks the ✔ means “this person is who they say they are”: it is about identity. On Rilup the seal is about the content: this was captured live. So we do not borrow a symbol. We use our own mark, which is already a fingerprint. One symbol, two states: the mark lights up.

verified

The capture passed verification the moment it was taken. Everything published on Rilup carries it: there is no content without a seal.

Real Confirmed

Someone doubted it, reported it, and human review confirmed it was real. Being accused and coming out verified is not a punishment: it is the medal for having done something incredible.

Transparency

You can add on top. Never change what was captured.

Cuts, text, hashtags, crop, exposure, the look: all of that exists on Rilup, and all of it is stored as a declared layer applied at playback. The attested file is never touched: it stays intact, byte for byte.

The consequence is the part that matters: anyone can remove the layers. Press and hold “View original” on any post and you see the capture exactly as it left the sensor. No makeup, and no need to take our word for it.

What is forbidden: filters or retouching that alter the captured reality: changing faces or bodies, removing or adding objects, any generative alteration. It is in the Terms, and a confirmed violation costs your level and your account.

The rules of the game

Everything exists for one reason.

That what you see here is real, and that you do not have to take our word for it.

Everything is captured here, live.

Nothing can be imported: not from the camera roll, not from a file, not from another app.

Built for the attempt.

The amazing never comes out on the first try. Record as many times as you need and publish the best take: every attempt is verified from the moment of capture.

Trust is earned with a track record, not with likes.

Your Reality Level rises by publishing verified captures over time. Never with followers or popularity. And it cannot be bought.

No automated system punishes on its own.

Every sanction goes through human review, and can be appealed. A detector can raise its hand; it cannot be the judge.

Your feed is yours.

Chronological, only from the people you follow. No algorithm decides what you deserve to see. If you want to discover, you go to Explore, and there the interests you declare are in charge.
