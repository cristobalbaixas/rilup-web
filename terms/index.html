Terms of Service and Authenticity Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · English version published October 29, 2026 · Rilup SpA (Chile) · admin@rilup.com

By using Rilup you accept these terms. Rilup exists for one very specific purpose: that what is real can be proven. Everything else is subordinate to that.

1. The authenticity policy (the essentials)

It is forbidden to:

  1. Post content generated or altered by artificial intelligence.
  2. Film another screen (a monitor, a TV, another phone, a projector) to pass that content off as your own capture. A screen appearing incidentally in the scene is normal; the screen being the content is a violation.
  3. Pass off someone else's captures as your own, including sharing account credentials to post third-party material.

Every capture is verified at the source: its cryptographic fingerprint and an integrity verdict about the device are recorded at the exact moment of capture. Content cannot be imported from the gallery or from any external file.

2. Allowed edits

You can add on top (text, hashtags, mentions) and adjust the presentation (crop, exposure, choosing which segments to publish). The original file is never altered: edits are declared and applied at playback, and anyone can see the capture without them with the "View original" button. Not allowed are filters or retouching that alter the content of the captured reality (modifying faces or bodies, removing or adding objects, or any generative alteration).

3. Consequences of violations

Violations are always confirmed through human review (no automatic system sanctions on its own) and we always notify you inside the app, explaining what happened and why. There are two kinds of violation, with different consequences.

3.1 Authenticity

Posting AI-generated content, filming another screen, or altering what was captured. It is the one rule Rilup cannot relax, and posting it is never an accident.

  • First time: the post is hidden, your Reality Level goes back to zero and your account is suspended, with a notice on your profile during that period.
  • Second time: permanent suspension of the account and blocking of the device.

3.2 Content

Material that does not meet the community rules (section 6). Here we are deliberately more flexible, because it is usually something that slipped in unintentionally.

  • Most often only the post is taken down and we notify you, without sanction.
  • If it is blatant or repeated, there may be a recorded warning and, depending on severity, a temporary suspension.
  • The gravest content (material involving minors, terrorism) means immediate permanent suspension, with no prior ladder, and a report to the authorities where appropriate.

Your Reality Level is not affected by content violations. That indicator measures whether what you post is real, not your conduct, and mixing the two would make it less useful for everyone.

3.3 Preventive removal

When an automatic filter detects possibly forbidden material with high confidence, the post may be hidden immediately while a person reviews it. That is not a sanction: it generates no warning, it does not affect your account or your level, and if the review concludes it was fine, it is restored.

You have the right to appeal by writing to admin@rilup.com; the appeal is reviewed by a different person or at a different time.

4. Reality Level

It is a trust indicator earned by posting verified captures and keeping a clean record over time. It cannot be bought, it is not obtained through popularity, and it grants no rights over other users. It may be recalculated or adjusted.

5. Your content

The content you post is yours. You grant us a non-exclusive license to host it, to display it inside Rilup and on the public pages of the posts you share. You can delete your posts or your account whenever you want.

6. Conduct

Harassment, hate speech, sexually explicit content, graphic violence, illegal content, and posting identifiable people without their consent where it is reasonable to expect it are forbidden. Attempting to circumvent the verification mechanisms (emulators, modified devices, altered apps) is forbidden.

7. Brands and sponsored content

Commercial accounts are governed by the same authenticity rules: their content must be captured live and verified. All sponsored content is identified as such.

8. Service and liability

Rilup is offered "as is", under active development. We verify the origin of captures with the best tools available, but we do not guarantee that the system is impossible to fool: we guarantee that doing so is costly, detectable and sanctionable. We are not liable for content posted by users, within the limits allowed by law.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Chile.
