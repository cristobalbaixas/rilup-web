Rilup beta access

Rilup is a social network where only content captured live, with the in-app camera, can be published. Every capture is verified on the device at the moment it is taken. No uploads, no imports, no AI. Rilup SpA, Chile · admin@rilup.com

Try it on Android

Rilup is in closed beta on Google Play. No password or invitation code is needed, just a Google account to install it and any email address to sign up.

  1. Join the tester group with your Google account: https://groups.google.com/g/rilup-beta
  2. On your Android phone, open https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701343888623675464 and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Rilup from Google Play and sign up with your email. You will receive a one-time code, there is no password.
The app is in English when your phone is in English, and you can switch languages in Settings. Everything you see in the feed was captured live with the in-app camera and verified by the server. Press and hold ORIGINAL on any post to see the capture exactly as it left the sensor.

If you only have an iPhone

The iOS version is in development. Write to admin@rilup.com and we will walk you through the Android app.
